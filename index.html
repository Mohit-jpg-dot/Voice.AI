Questions?

How is Voice.AI different from traditional speech recognition? ▾

Traditional dictation only transcribes your literal speech word-for-word, including verbal fillers ("um", "uh"), awkward formatting errors, and typos. Voice.AI is an active AI voice operating system powered by on-device Whisper models and contextual LLM post-processing. It understands your intent, removes verbal clutter, and formats thoughts in real time. Beyond typing, Agent Mode turns spoken commands directly into actions—like summarizing email threads, scheduling meetings, creating Linear tickets, and automating multi-step tasks across your Mac.

Is my data private and secure with Voice.AI? ▾

Yes, completely. Voice.AI is built privacy-first: your audio is transcribed on-device using local Apple Silicon-optimized Whisper models. Your voice recordings are never stored on our servers and are never used to train public AI models. All network communication utilizes FIPS-compliant TLS 1.2+ encryption, and you have complete toggle-level control over what leaves your device. What you say stays yours.

What apps does Voice.AI work with? ▾

Voice.AI works with every application on your computer. In Dictation Mode, placing your cursor into any text field—whether in Slack, Notion, Gmail, VS Code, Obsidian, Apple Notes, or Google Docs—instantly inputs clean, formatted text. In Agent Mode, Voice.AI features deep native integrations with over 20+ applications (including Google Calendar, Linear, Jira, Apple Mail, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Chrome) plus Model Context Protocol (MCP) server support for custom developer tooling.
