## 1. The "Point Anywhere" Screen Superpower
You no longer have to explain context, take screenshots, or copy-paste text. **Your cursor is the context.** Point your mouse at anything on screen and speak:

* **Instant Person / Company Lookup:**
  * Point at someone’s photo on a website or social feed:
  * 🗣️ *"Find this person’s LinkedIn and open it."*
* **Explaining Errors & Code:**
  * Point at a red error in Xcode, Terminal, or a browser console:
  * 🗣️ *"Look at this error under my cursor. What does it mean and how do I fix it?"*
* **Document & Invoice Analysis:**
  * Point at a chart, PDF, or invoice on screen:
  * 🗣️ *"Summarize the key trends in this chart in 3 bullet points."*
  * 🗣️ *"Extract the subtotal, tax, and invoice number from this receipt."*
* **Clicking & Form Filling:**
  * 🗣️ *"Click on the blue 'Approve' button in this window."*
  * 🗣️ *"Fill in this address form with my standard shipping info."*
* **Interactive Screen Beacon (Visual Tutor):**
  * Don't know where a button is in complex software (Figma, Photoshop, Final Cut, Xcode)?
  * 🗣️ *"Where do I export an SVG in this window?"*
  * Voice.AI lights up a glowing, pulsing beacon directly over the physical button on your screen so you know exactly where to click.

1. Locate the file in Downloads via `SandboxFileManagerEngine`.

3. Open the In-Pill Email Composer with the lawyer’s address and pre-filled message.
4. Schedule the follow-up reminder in Apple Reminders.
5. Voice-report back when all steps are finished.

## 10. Safety & Privacy by Design

* **Zero-Strain on Your Mac:** Claude runs on cloud infrastructure. Your Mac's 16 GB unified RAM stays completely free for your own creative apps, with **zero fan noise and maximum battery life**.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation:** High-stakes actions (sending an email, deleting files, posting messages, executing shell commands) display an approval card in the notch. Nothing irreversible happens without your explicit consent.
* **Secret Redactor:** API keys, passwords, and tokens are encrypted in macOS Keychain and automatically redacted so they are never leaked in logs or prompts.
* **Anti-Prompt-Injection:** Web pages and incoming messages are treated as untrusted data, preventing malicious websites from hijacking your voice assistant.
